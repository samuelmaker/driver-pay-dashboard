# Driver Guide (How to use the Pay Dashboard)

This guide is for **drivers** using the Driver Pay Dashboard to check month‑to‑date hours and pay.

## What you need

- The Driver Pay Dashboard website link
- Your **name** (as it appears in the dropdown)
- Your **6‑digit PIN**

If you don’t have your PIN, contact your manager/admin.

## Logging in

1. Open the Driver Pay Dashboard link.
2. In **Select Your Name**, choose your name.
3. Enter your **6‑digit PIN**.
4. Tap/click **Login**.

You’ll land on the **Driver Dashboard**.

## Driver Dashboard tour

At the top you will see:

- A **month selector** (you can view the current month or previous months)
- A **Logout** button

### Pay Summary (top box)

This section shows:

- **Total Hours**: your hours for the selected month
- **Hourly Rate**: your pay rate per hour
- **Total Pay**: your pay for the selected month

#### Adjustments (if you see a note in brackets)

Sometimes an admin may correct your hours (for example: overtime, holiday pay, or a correction).  
If that happens you’ll see a note like:

- “(+2 hours adjusted: Overtime)”
- “(-0.5 hours adjusted: Duplicate route fix)”

That means:

- your hours were **calculated from your routes**, then
- an **adjustment** was added/removed by an admin for that month

### Detailed Breakdown (table)

This table shows each route for the month with:

- **Date**
- **Hours**
- **Route**

Some routes may show a status instead of hours:

- **Not Started**: the route hasn’t been started yet (so hours may be 0 / not counted yet).
- **In Progress**: the route has started but not completed yet (final hours may not be available yet).

If the route name has an arrow (↗), you can click it to open the route in Spoke (it opens in a new tab).

## Downloading your statement

Under the Pay Summary, you can download:

- **Download CSV**: a spreadsheet version of your statement
- **Download PDF**: a printable statement (your browser may ask you to allow pop‑ups)

Tip: If PDF doesn’t open, allow pop‑ups for the site and try again.

## Switching months

Use the month dropdown at the top right to select a different month.  
The dashboard will reload automatically with that month’s totals and routes.

## Logging out

Click **Logout** when you’re finished (especially on shared devices).

## Common problems (and what to do)

### “Login failed” / “invalid pin”

- Re‑enter the PIN carefully (6 digits).
- Make sure you selected the correct name.
- If it still fails, ask your admin to confirm your PIN.

### “Failed to fetch pay data” / nothing loads

- Try switching months and back again.
- Log out and log back in.
- If it keeps failing, report it to your admin (include the month you selected).

### “Please allow pop-ups to download PDF”

- Allow pop‑ups for the site in your browser settings, then try again.

### My hours look wrong

- Check you’re viewing the correct month.
- If you have routes that are “Not Started” or “In Progress”, they may not count fully yet.
- If you think it’s still wrong, tell your admin:
  - the month
  - the route date(s)
  - what looks incorrect
